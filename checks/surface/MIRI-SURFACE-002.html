MIRI-SURFACE-002 — Nests the payload; never merges publisher bytes into the envelope

Nests the payload; never merges publisher bytes into the envelope

must severity CRITICAL · 5 weight 8 Discovery Contract

Definition: standards/consumption/checks/MIRI-SURFACE-002.yaml · view · raw

Violation
{"schema_version": "1", "ok": false, "error": {...}, "identity": {...}} # publisher wrote ok/error into lifecycle.json and the surface merged it
Compliant
{"schema_version": "1", "ok": true, "present": true, "document": {"ok": false, "error": {...}, "identity": {...}}} # the publisher's keys stay inside document and mean nothing to the envelope

Why this matters

This is the clause the entire anti-forgery argument rests on. If publisher bytes are merged into the top level rather than nested under a payload key, a package can write ok, present or error into its own document and forge the surface's signals — the adversarial fixture does exactly that. Wrapping also avoids invalidating the document: the producer schemas set additionalProperties false, so injecting an envelope field would make a conforming document non-conforming.

Definition

Surface obligation from the Discovery Contract. This check verifies: Nests the payload; never merges publisher bytes into the envelope.

When it fires

  • The surface copies publisher-controlled bytes into its own reason field — document contents, an echo of an offending line from a parser, or a path drawn from package data. reason is surface-authored: the permitted variable parts are values the surface computed or received from the consumer. A field carrying arbitrary publisher text into the envelope is an injection channel wearing a human-readable label, which is this check's own defect one field over
  • A transport-layer message the binding emits on the surface's behalf quotes a publisher string — the same bytes through a different door
  • A payload document's keys appear at the envelope's top level
  • The surface injects schema_version or any envelope field into the payload document
  • The surface hoists a payload field into the envelope

How to fix it

Nest every payload under its operation's payload key: document, entries, packages, patterns, graph, resolution

  1. Nest every payload under its operation's payload key: document, entries, packages, patterns, graph, resolution
  2. Never mutate the payload document — relay the bytes as parsed, adding nothing and removing nothing
