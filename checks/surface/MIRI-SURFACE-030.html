MIRI-SURFACE-030 — Declares and enforces its caps, and sets `truncated` when it drops content

Declares and enforces its caps, and sets truncated when it drops content

must severity MEDIUM · 3 weight 6 Discovery Contract

Definition: standards/consumption/checks/MIRI-SURFACE-030.yaml · view · raw

Violation
{"entries": {...25 of 2060...}, "ok": true} # no cap, no truncated
Compliant
{"schema_version": "1", "ok": true, "present": true, "cap": 25, "truncated": true, "next_cursor": "…", "entries": {...}}

Why this matters

A consumer cannot tell a complete answer from a cut one unless the surface says. Silence here is what lets a hostile package hide a symbol by padding its index past the cap: the consumer sees a plausible list, no truncation flag, and concludes the missing surface does not exist.

Definition

Surface obligation from the Discovery Contract. This check verifies: Declares and enforces its caps, and sets truncated when it drops content.

When it fires

  • A capped operation returns without cap
  • Entries or bytes are dropped without truncated: true
  • More than cap entries are returned

How to fix it

Report cap on every response from list, api-index, patterns, graph, and document

  1. Report cap on every response from list, api-index, patterns and graph; document is bounded by bytes rather than entries and reports max_bytes instead
  2. Set truncated: true whenever anything was dropped, including when the surface cannot continue
