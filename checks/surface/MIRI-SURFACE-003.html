MIRI-SURFACE-003 — Emits only reserved keys at the top level

Emits only reserved keys at the top level

must severity HIGH · 4 weight 6 Discovery Contract

Definition: standards/consumption/checks/MIRI-SURFACE-003.yaml · view · raw

Violation

Compliant
{"schema_version": "1", "ok": true, "present": true, "document": {...}}

Why this matters

The un-forgeability argument needs the surface to own a *known, closed* key set. A surface that adds its own extra top-level fields erodes that: a consumer cannot tell an unfamiliar key from a forged one, and the reserved-field table stops being exhaustive in practice while still claiming to be.

Definition

Surface obligation from the Discovery Contract. This check verifies: Emits only reserved keys at the top level.

When it fires

  • A response carries a top-level key outside the reserved set in §4.1

How to fix it

Emit only the reserved fields; put anything else inside the payload
