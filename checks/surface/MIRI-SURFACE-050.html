MIRI-SURFACE-050 — Advertises the surface version at the normative path

Advertises the surface version at the normative path

must severity MEDIUM · 3 weight 4 Discovery Contract

Definition: standards/consumption/checks/MIRI-SURFACE-050.yaml · view · raw

Violation
{"protocolVersion": "2024-11-05", "serverInfo": {"name": "miri", "version": "1.0"}}
Compliant
{"protocolVersion": "2024-11-05", "capabilities": {"miri": {"surface_version": "1"}}, "serverInfo": {"name": "miri", "version": "1.0"}}

Why this matters

The transport's protocol version names the transport, and serverInfo.version names the implementation. Neither names the *contract*. Without a distinct surface version a client cannot negotiate the metadata contract independently of the transport it happens to ride, which is the whole reason the contract is transport-agnostic.

Definition

Surface obligation from the Discovery Contract. This check verifies: Advertises the surface version at the normative path.

When it fires

  • initialize advertises no surface version at the normative path
  • The surface version is conflated with the transport protocol version or with serverInfo.version

How to fix it

Advertise the surface version at capabilities.miri.surface_version as a string

  1. Advertise the surface version at capabilities.miri.surface_version as a string
