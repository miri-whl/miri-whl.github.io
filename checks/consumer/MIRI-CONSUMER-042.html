MIRI-CONSUMER-042 — Never presents a whole-document retrieval as a derived view

Never presents a whole-document retrieval as though a derived view had been used

should severity LOW · 1 weight 1 Discovery Contract §3.2.3

Definition: standards/consumption/checks/MIRI-CONSUMER-042.yaml · view · raw

Violation
document(package="pandas", name="sdk-manifest.json") # 400 KB retrieved to find one symbol
Compliant
api-index(package="pandas", query="read_csv") # routing view, capped

Why this matters

This is a budget preference, not a correctness rule, and is graded separately as such. Retrieving a large document to use one part of it is the behavior the derived views were added to make unnecessary.

Definition

Budget preference, graded separately from the correctness MUSTs. Restated as an output claim because which operation a consumer called is a request-side fact the profile's verification model cannot observe (§2); what it can observe is the claim the consumer makes about what it read.

When it fires

  • The consumer's output claims a filtered or routing view was used when the whole document was retrieved
  • The consumer reports a document's full contents as though they were a scoped answer to the question asked

How to fix it

Use api-index, patterns, or graph where the question is a routing, pattern, or relationship question
